Design oversights are apparent.  The "cellgrid" object doesn't just hold the
grid cells: it also holds the labels!  It seems to even hold residual objects
that were freed!  It is not feasible to assume a 1-to-1 relationship between
cell grid positions, and the actual cell positions on the map.

Therefore a manual tracking of the cells themselves must be acquired; you
can't simply call "get_children()" on the cell grid to get the nodes; you have
to store a list of the cells themselves manually at generation.
